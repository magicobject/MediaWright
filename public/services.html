Services

What we can build for you.

Three kinds of project. The approach is the same for each: understand what you actually need, build it plainly and well, and leave you able to run it yourself.

Business

Sites for businesses

If you're a tutor, tradesperson, consultant or small operation, your site's job is simple: convince someone to get in touch. We build for that — clear, fast, and set up to be found in local search.

  • A site built around getting enquiries, not just looking nice
  • Fast-loading, mobile-friendly, no bloated page-builder underneath
  • Structured data and local SEO groundwork built in from the start

See this in practice: Wright Maths Tuition →

Charity & community

Sites for charities, churches & community groups

Community organisations need sites that are cheap to run, accessible to everyone, and simple enough for a volunteer — not a developer — to keep updated. That's what we build.

  • Free or at-cost, depending on what your group can afford
  • Low running costs, no ongoing software fees
  • Accessible by default, not as an afterthought
  • Easy for a non-technical volunteer to edit day to day

Every community site we build uses the same templated, tested and versioned build pipeline as our paid client work — nothing stripped down. And the code is yours from day one: it lives in a public repo on GitHub, so if you ever want to move hosting, bring in another developer, or just see how it works, you can — no permission needed, no lock-in.

See this in practice: Kington Foodbank →

Writing & books

Turning a book into a website

A book deserves more than a PDF link. We build proper websites for books and writing projects — every chapter genuinely readable and findable, alongside clear ways to download or buy.

  • The whole book actually present on the page, not hidden behind clicks
  • Built to be found by search, not just shared once on social media
  • Download and purchase links front and centre
