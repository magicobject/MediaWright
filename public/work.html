Proof of concept

Kington Parishes

A proof-of-concept rebuild of a five-church benefice website. Started as a straightforward parish site; became the clearest demonstration of the whole approach — a header/footer redesign driven by a one-line piece of feedback ("looks rather grey"), resolved with a gradient whose every colour pairing was checked by relative-luminance calculation rather than eyeballed; a decorative hand-drawn sketch turned into a working, accessible navigation feature; structured data and what3words wayfinding added for a rural benefice that's genuinely hard to find. Every change went through the full test suite and an accessibility audit before it shipped — nothing exempted because it was "just a demo." This is a demo, not the parishes' actual website, and it's kept out of search results so it's never confused with the genuine one.
