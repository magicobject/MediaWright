Wright Maths Tuition

A site for a Leominster-based maths tutor offering one-to-one lessons from GCSE through to degree level, in person and online. Built to convert visitors into enquiries, rank for local search terms like “maths tutor Leominster”, and make safeguarding practice genuinely clear to parents — not just a line in the small print.

Charity & community

Kington Foodbank

A straightforward site for a local foodbank — opening times, what's needed, and how to get help or give it, all easy to find and easy to keep current without any technical know-how. Built free of charge, with the code openly available for the foodbank to keep, move, or hand to anyone they choose.
